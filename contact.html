Common Questions

Before you reach out

How do I place a bid on an original?

Fill out the contact form with "Purchase / Bid on Original" selected. Include the artwork name and your maximum offer. We'll confirm the current reserve and guide you through the process.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes — prints and originals ship worldwide. Prints are shipped flat-packed in rigid mailers. Originals are professionally packed, insured, and tracked. Shipping costs are calculated at checkout.

How long do commissions take?

Typically 8–14 weeks from deposit to delivery, depending on size and complexity. Juliana accepts a limited number of commissions each year, so early enquiry is recommended.

Can I see the work in person before buying?

Absolutely. Original works available in NYC can be viewed by appointment. The virtual gallery also lets you zoom into brushstroke-level detail. Contact us to arrange an in-person viewing.

What's included with an original painting?

Every original comes with a signed Certificate of Authenticity, care instructions, the work's backstory in writing, and professional packing. Framing is available as an add-on.

How do I host a pop-up exhibition?

We partner with venues in NYC and select cities. If you have a space — a gallery, hotel lobby, private home, or event venue — and would like to host a Sophia Studios pop-up, tell us about it via the contact form.
